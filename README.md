# BooHoo

This project originates from the Jame Gam Game Jam program at HackClub, with the theme "I'm Just a Little Guy", and a tiny useless ghost fits it as this ghost is small and little! This ghost (or the little guy) is trying to scare a human who is sleeping in this game.

## How to play and about the project
Use WASD or the arrow keys to move, use E to interact with objects, use shift to sprint, and use Q to let out a quick squeak! Your goal is to fill the scare meter by using different scare combos to scare the human, then say "BOO!" using E at the human once the scare meter is full. Make sure you don't use the same move twice or scare while the detection bar is red! If you are new to the game, when you first enter the game, you can choose to play the tutorial, which will walk you through all the main game functions so you aren't lost in the real gameplay! It will also show you all the special challenges that come up in later nights.

You can press escape or press the 2 grey lines in the top right corner of the game screen if you would like to pause your game at any time, go to any other nights which you have beaten, or just to restart the current level that you are on. There are 10 levels (referred to as "nights") in this game. Each night, you must build up the scare meter high enough until you really scare the human. Once you finally win a night, you can either move on to the next night or replay the current night (if you would like a better time score on that night). Each night gets harder as you go with increased difficulties and challenges you must surpass like a timer you must scare the person within, a limited amount of "scares" (things you do to scare the human), creaky floorboards that increase the detection meter, a burned out lamp (lamp will not work after it is burned out), and the human tossing and turning to increase the detection meter. 

The game does remember your progress even if you refresh or close the tab. Data is saved into the browser, so as long as you do not clear your browser data, it will not reset you. When you beat night 10... a special thing happens, but no spoilers! AI was used for creating the original project structure and boilerplate to help me get started, resolving merge conflicts, helped me with the math and physics functions, helped me find free assets online, and helped me with the brainstorming of some feature ideas to add.

This project was built using TypeScript, Vite, and HTML canvas. You can visit and play the game at https://playboohoo.vercel.app/

## Quick list of things you can scare with:
- the lamp on the cabinet (before night 6)
- the toy on the shelf (and the floor once you knock it off)
- the ball on the floor
- your squeaky "boo" (press q)
- the bookshelf
- the window

Combos give you extra points on the SCARE bar, but you must do it quick enough before the ring around the ghost goes away:
- the lamp, then the ball
- the toy, then your squeak
- the window, then the bookshelf
